## Less guessing. Follow the evidence.

An answer can lead to research, a file, or a change to your server. You can redirect the work as it happens.

> **You**: Why did attendance drop at our last event? Check the discussion, not just the announcements.
>
> **Sophia**: I'll read the event threads and compare the reasons people gave. [Stop] [Details]
>
> **You**: Sophia, focus on scheduling conflicts in the event review. Leave prizes out of this.
>
> **Sophia**: I'll focus on the date and time, and separate confirmed reasons from guesses.
